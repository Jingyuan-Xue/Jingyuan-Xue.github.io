I am a graduate student in the Master of Information Technology program at UNSW Sydney and an incoming Research Master's student at Macquarie University. My work focuses on building practical LLM applications, especially retrieval-augmented generation systems, HybridRAG architectures, fine-tuning workflows, and full-stack AI products.

Recently, I worked as an LLM Application Development Intern at Laikon Biotech, where I built data governance and model evaluation pipelines and fine-tuned Qwen-based models for domain-specific workflows. I also worked as a Full Stack Engineer at CitiXP, contributing to cross-platform product development, authentication integration, app releases, and performance-oriented infrastructure migration.

I am interested in the engineering side of LLM systems: connecting model capability with reliable data pipelines, graph and vector retrieval, tool use, and user-facing applications. You can explore my selected work on the [Projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) page and find a structured overview of my background on the [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}) page.
